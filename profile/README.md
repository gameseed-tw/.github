## About GameSeed

**GameSeed is an emerging initiative for game development education, talent cultivation, and creative collaboration.**

We believe that every game creator begins as a seed. With opportunities to learn, experiment, and connect, those seeds can grow into teams, games, communities, and future mentors.

Initiated at the **Department of Digital Content Design, Ling Tung University, Taiwan**, GameSeed aims to grow beyond a single campus. Our long-term vision is an open international network where educators, students, independent developers, and industry communities share knowledge and help one another create games.

## 🌿 The SEED Philosophy

| Pillar | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **S — Skills** | Develop practical skills in game design, development, and production. |
| **E — Experimentation** | Explore ideas through prototypes, creative challenges, and hands-on projects. |
| **E — Exchange** | Connect educators, students, creators, and industry communities across borders. |
| **D — Dissemination** | Share reusable learning resources and experience so new creators can grow elsewhere. |

## 🎮 What We Hope to Grow

Our planned areas of work include:

- **Open game-development education:** accessible learning materials, tools, and project templates.
- **Learning by making:** playable prototypes, collaborative projects, and Game Jam participation.
- **Mentorship and incubation:** pathways from first skills to finished projects and sustainable teams.
- **Educator and community exchange:** connections among schools, independent developers, and game-industry communities.
- **A network of seeds:** opportunities for learners to become mentors and help new communities take root.

## 🤝 Starting in Taiwan, Growing Together

Our starting point is **Ling Tung University's Department of Digital Content Design** and its developing **Game Development Training Center** (遊戲製作培訓中心).

The initiative also draws on experience in Taiwan's independent game-development community, including long-standing exchanges with **TiGG**. As GameSeed develops, we hope to invite more educators, campuses, creators, and communities in Taiwan and internationally to participate.

Our goal is not only to help people make games, but also to help them pass their skills, creative confidence, and opportunities on to others.
